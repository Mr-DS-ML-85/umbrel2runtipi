# Invio

Invio is a self-hosted invoicing app without the bloat

Invio is a self-hosted invoicing without the bloat. A fast, minimalist, and privacy-friendly invoicing system built for freelancers and small teams who want control, not clutter.

Create and share invoices in seconds, host your data yourself, and share secure, password-free links with clients. Lightweight by design, no CRM overhead and no SaaS lock-in.
